How it works

From one memo to a plan you approve

Follow one capture from the first word to your day.

  1. 1 Talk

    Talk it out.

    Tap the mic and speak the way you think — “Pick up the cake tomorrow at 9, Maya’s party is at 3 at Dolores Park, and buy a gift card.”

    • Record up to 2 minutes free, or up to 20 minutes with Pro.
    • Start a memo from the Home Screen widget, Control Center, Siri or the Action Button.
    • Record in 15 languages. English recordings show a live transcript as you talk.

  2. 2 Type

    Or type it.

    When talking isn’t an option, type the same memo. WhisperAct sorts typed memos the same way and shows you what it found.

  3. 3 Review

    It asks when it’s not sure.

    WhisperAct splits your memo into reminders, events and to-dos. When a date is vague, like “this month”, it asks which day instead of guessing — and a phrase like “every morning” is confirmed before a repeat is set.

    • Switch any item between to-do, reminder and event in one tap.
    • Fix the wording, date or place, add what it missed, or leave an item out.
    • Every capture opens for review by default. You can turn on auto-save for clear items later; anything unclear still asks.

  4. 4 Plan

    See what’s ahead.

    The Plan tab keeps to-dos, reminders and events together in Today, Upcoming and Inbox, with a countdown to what’s next. Memos keeps every capture with its transcript and items.

    • Long-press an item in Plan for quick date choices.
    • Filter by Personal and Work categories.

  5. 5 Day

    A calm daily rhythm.

    The Day tab holds Daily Review — a quick pass over what’s due and what still needs a decision — plus an evening wrap-up and the times your words mean, like when your “morning” starts.

    • A Today widget for your Home Screen, with a mic to start a memo, or your Lock Screen.
    • Daily Review, the Daily Digest notification and widgets are free for everyone.

Apple Reminders & Calendar

Saved in Apple’s apps, or kept in WhisperAct.

Every capture has a destination, shown at the top of the review. With Apple apps selected, reminders go to Apple Reminders and events to Apple Calendar, so they show up wherever those apps do. Choose WhisperAct and items stay in the app until you send them from the item’s menu.

  • Free: 7 Apple Reminders and 7 Apple Calendar saves each month. If you reach a limit, items stay in WhisperAct and can be synced later.
  • Pro: unlimited saves, plus a specific Reminders list or calendar of your choice.

Features

Built around how you actually talk

The details that keep a quick memo from turning into cleanup later.

  • 15 recording languages

    English, Español, Français, Português, Deutsch, Italiano, Polski, Türkçe, Русский, Ελληνικά, العربية, اردو, Bahasa Indonesia, 한국어 and 日本語. Items come back in the language you spoke.

    Recording in a language other than English needs an internet connection. The app itself is in English and Spanish.

  • Repeats, understood

    Say “take vitamins every morning at 8” and WhisperAct suggests a daily repeat for you to confirm, then rolls the date forward each time you’re done.

    Free for everyone. With Pro, repeats on items synced to Apple also become native recurring series.

  • Reminders at a place Pro

    “Remind me when I get to the pharmacy” sets a place instead of a time. Apple Reminders delivers it when you arrive or leave, even with WhisperAct closed.

  • Share it your way

    Share any memo as plain text or Markdown, free. With Pro, export a polished PDF or several memos at once.

  • Your words, your times

    In the Day tab, tell WhisperAct when your day starts and ends and what “morning” or “evening” means to you, so spoken times land where you expect.

  • Offline now, smarter later

    No connection? English recording still works on devices with Apple’s on-device speech recognition, and items are found on your device. Re-Analyze with Cloud AI when you’re back online.

    Re-Analyze: 7 a month free, unlimited with Pro.

Privacy

No account. No ads. Clear answers about your data.

WhisperAct has no servers of its own for your memos. Here’s where your words go.

On your device

  • Memos and items are stored on your iPhone or iPad, and in your private iCloud database if you have Pro.
  • English recordings are transcribed by Apple’s speech recognition, on-device on supported devices.

Sent for processing

  • Recordings in other languages are transcribed by Groq.
  • When you’re online, the text of your memo — not the audio — is sent to OpenAI, with Cerebras as a backup, to find tasks and dates.
  • Requests carry no name or Apple ID, but they include whatever you said or typed.

Never for advertising

  • No ads and no cross-app tracking.
  • Analytics and crash reports are limited, and WhisperAct doesn’t add your memo content to them.
  • We never sell your personal data.
