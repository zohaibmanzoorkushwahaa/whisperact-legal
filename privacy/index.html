Privacy Policy

Your privacy,
protected.

Last updated: October 21, 2026  ·  Effective: August 18, 2026  ·  Questions? support@whisperact.com

No accounts. No ads. No cross-app tracking. We never sell your personal data. WhisperAct has no servers of its own for your content. Your memos live on your device and in your private iCloud account — not ours. Online transcription and AI extraction send content to the processing providers described below.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how WhisperAct ("we", "us", or "the App") handles information when you use the WhisperAct iOS application. By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

WhisperAct is designed with a minimal data footprint. The App does not create user accounts or operate proprietary servers for your content. It uses Firebase Analytics for limited product metrics and Firebase Crashlytics for crash diagnostics. Those services never receive your memo content through WhisperAct's custom events, and they are not used for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Who we are. WhisperAct is operated by Zohaib Manzoor Kushwaha, Lahore, Punjab, Pakistan. For the purposes of the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), we act as the data controller for the limited processing described in this policy. You can reach us at any time at support@whisperact.com.

2. Information Kept Out of Analytics

WhisperAct does not deliberately send or attach the following to Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics:

  • Your name, email address, or any contact information
  • Memo audio, transcripts, titles, notes, task text, or calendar content
  • Place names, saved-place coordinates, or exact reminder and event times
  • Advertising IDs (IDFA) or Apple's vendor identifier (IDFV)
  • Contacts, photos, or any data outside the App's scope

The App contains no advertising SDK and does not use analytics data to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

3. Voice Recordings

Audio is recorded entirely on your device using your device's microphone. The audio file is used solely to produce a text transcript and is deleted immediately after transcription completes. WhisperAct does not upload, retain, or transmit your raw audio to any server operated by WhisperAct.

For non-English recordings (any of the supported languages other than English), the audio file and the selected language are transmitted to Groq's speech-to-text API (api.groq.com) for transcription. Groq processes the audio and returns a text transcript. Groq's published data policy states that it does not retain inference data by default. Without further settings, Groq may log inputs and outputs for up to 30 days to troubleshoot reliability errors or investigate suspected abuse. Since October 2026, WhisperAct has enabled Groq's Zero Data Retention setting for the Groq organizations the App uses; under that setting, Groq states that it does not retain customer data for reliability or abuse monitoring either. See also Groq's Privacy Policy.

4. Transcription

Transcription is handled differently depending on your language selection and device:

  • English on supported iOS 26 devices: Transcription runs entirely on-device using Apple's on-device speech engine (SpeechTranscriber). Your audio never leaves your device in this path.
  • English on other devices or as a fallback: Transcription may use Apple's speech recognition servers — the same infrastructure that powers Siri and Dictation — governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. WhisperAct does not receive, store, or process the audio in this path; it is handled end-to-end by Apple.
  • Non-English languages (Urdu, Arabic, Spanish, French, Portuguese, German, Greek, Turkish, Russian, Indonesian, Italian, Polish, Korean, Japanese): The audio file is sent to Groq's Whisper API for transcription when an internet connection is available. Groq's data handling is described in Section 3. Transcription is unavailable for these languages when offline.

5. Task and Event Extraction (AI)

When you are online, the text you submit or the transcript — not the audio — is sent to AI processing providers to identify tasks, reminders, and calendar events. In version 4.0.0 and later, WhisperAct uses OpenAI and Cerebras to turn submitted text or transcripts into tasks, reminders, and events. Cerebras may process a request when OpenAI cannot complete it.

In these versions, an extraction request contains the submitted text or transcript, processing instructions and a response format, the current date and time, your time zone, language information, and your configured time preferences. For some requests, WhisperAct also asks OpenAI to verify the result; that request contains the original transcript, candidate titles, and schedule summaries. WhisperAct has no user accounts and does not add your name or Apple ID to these requests. However, memo content can include personal information you choose to say or type, such as names, places, or appointment details, and that content is sent as part of the request. As with any internet request, providers also receive technical connection data such as your IP address.

OpenAI. In WhisperAct's OpenAI organization, sharing data with OpenAI for model training is disabled, and the App sends extraction requests with response storage turned off (store: false), so OpenAI does not keep the response as stored application state. These settings do not mean zero retention. OpenAI's standard abuse-monitoring retention applies: under its API data policy, OpenAI keeps abuse-monitoring logs, which can include prompts and responses, for up to 30 days, unless longer retention is required by law or reasonably necessary to protect its services or others from harm.

Cerebras. Cerebras states that it does not retain prompts, API requests, or responses, and keeps only account and usage information needed to operate its service. See also the Cerebras Privacy Policy.

Earlier versions. In versions earlier than 4.0.0, online extraction sent the transcript and date and time context to Groq's language model API, and some requests were processed by Fireworks AI instead. Groq's data handling is described in Section 3.

When you are offline, or as a fallback, AI extraction runs entirely on-device using Apple Intelligence or a built-in keyword analysis engine. No data leaves your device in this path.

How we use these providers. Groq, OpenAI, and Cerebras receive this content only so they can perform the transcription or extraction you request and return the result to the App. They act as WhisperAct's service providers, under their own terms and the retention practices described in Sections 3, 5, and 9. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it with these or any other third parties for advertising or profiling. We use only providers whose terms provide protection for this data equal to or greater than the protection described in this policy.

6. Memos, Tasks, and Calendar Data

All memos and tasks are stored locally on your device using SwiftData. If you subscribe to WhisperAct Pro, your data is synced to your private iCloud database via Apple's CloudKit framework. This data is stored in your personal Apple account and is not accessible to us. WhisperAct has no access to your iCloud content.

Reminders and calendar events are written directly to your device via Apple's EventKit framework and remain fully under your control within Apple's Reminders and Calendar applications.

7. Analytics and Crash Diagnostics

Firebase Analytics. On physical devices in release builds, the App records a small set of product events using bounded labels, such as capture input type and AI engine, manual-item type and screen, Apple-sync failure category, review outcome, and paywall context. Custom events never include free text or memo content. Firebase also automatically processes app lifecycle and product-interaction events, an app-instance identifier, device/OS/app metadata, a broad geographic region derived from a masked IP address, and App Store purchase or subscription events.

IDFV collection and advertising-personalisation signals are disabled. The App does not link AdSupport, does not access IDFA, and does not use Firebase data for advertising or cross-app tracking. Analytics collection is disabled in Debug builds and in the iOS Simulator.

Firebase Crashlytics. Crashlytics sends crash stack traces, app and device state, OS/app versions, a Firebase installation identifier, and related diagnostic data to help us find and fix crashes. We do not set a user ID or deliberately attach memo, transcript, task, note, place, or schedule content to crash reports.

Firebase is operated by Google. See Firebase Privacy and Security for its processing and retention information.

8. Subscriptions and Purchases

All subscription purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. WhisperAct does not receive payment card information or billing details. The App receives subscription transaction and product status needed to unlock Pro, and Firebase Analytics may automatically record purchase or subscription events as described above.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

WhisperAct stores your memo and task content only on your device and, for Pro users, in your private iCloud account. We do not maintain copies of that content on any proprietary server. Limited analytics and crash-diagnostic data is retained by Google under Firebase's policies and configured retention controls. Content sent for online transcription or AI extraction is handled by those providers under the retention terms described in Sections 3, 5, and 9.

To delete all your data, go to Settings → Delete All Data within the App. This permanently removes all memos and tasks from your device and iCloud. Deleting or reinstalling the App from your device also removes all locally stored data. Deleting content in WhisperAct does not automatically delete records that a processing provider may hold under its own retention terms.

11. Children's Privacy

WhisperAct is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the EU/EEA, or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child below the applicable age has used the App, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

12. Your Rights & EU / EEA / UK Data Protection (GDPR)

WhisperAct keeps data on your device and in your private iCloud account, and does not store your memos, transcripts, or audio on any server we operate. The limited online processing described in this policy is covered below for users in the EU, EEA, and UK.

Legal bases for processing. Where the GDPR applies, we rely on:

  • Performance of a contract — to provide the transcription and task-extraction features you actively request.
  • Legitimate interests — to operate, secure, and maintain the App. We use no advertising, profiling, or behavioral tracking.

International data transfers. When transcription (non-English) or AI task extraction is performed online, the relevant audio, text, or transcript is processed by the providers described in Sections 3, 5, and 9: Groq for transcription, and OpenAI and Cerebras for extraction in version 4.0.0 and later (Groq and Fireworks AI in earlier versions). Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics data is processed by Google. These services may process data outside the EU/EEA/UK, including in the United States, under their applicable data-transfer terms. See the provider links in Section 9 and Firebase Privacy and Security for details.

Your rights. You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, object to, and port your personal data. Because your memo and task content lives on your device and in your own iCloud account, you can exercise most of these rights directly in the App — edit or delete any item, or use Settings → Delete All Data (see Section 10). This does not delete records a processing provider may hold under its own retention terms. For analytics/diagnostic requests or anything else we can help with, email support@whisperact.com.

Right to complain. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority in the EU, EEA, or UK.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Your continued use of the App after any such changes constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

14. Contact

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@whisperact.com.

This Privacy Policy applies to all versions of WhisperAct available on the Apple App Store.
