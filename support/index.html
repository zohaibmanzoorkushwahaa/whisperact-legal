Getting started

How do I start recording?

Tap the microphone button at the bottom of the screen to open the recorder, then tap the mic and speak naturally. The first time, WhisperAct asks for access to the microphone and, for English, speech recognition. Tap stop when you’re done: free recordings can be up to 2 minutes, and Pro recordings up to 20 minutes. WhisperAct then shows the items it found so you can review them.

Prefer typing? Tap Type a memo instead on the recording screen. You can also start a memo from the WhisperAct Today widget, Control Center, Siri or the Action Button.

What languages does WhisperAct support?

You can record in 15 languages: English, Urdu (اردو), Arabic (العربية), Spanish, French, Portuguese, German, Greek (Ελληνικά), Turkish, Russian, Indonesian, Italian, Polish, Korean and Japanese (日本語). Choose the language with the language button (for example EN) at the top of the recording screen before you record.

English is transcribed by Apple’s speech recognition, on-device on supported devices. The other languages are transcribed online by Groq, so they need an internet connection. Tasks, reminders and event titles come back in the language you spoke, with any English words you mixed in kept as you said them. The app’s own screens are in English and Spanish.

Does WhisperAct work without an internet connection?

Partly. Recording in English works offline on iPhones and iPads that support Apple’s on-device speech recognition; on other devices, English recording needs a connection. Recording in any other language always needs a connection. Typed memos work offline.

Offline, items are found on your device — by Apple Intelligence for English where it’s available, otherwise by a built-in basic engine — so results can be simpler. You can Re-Analyze the memo with Cloud AI once you’re back online. With Pro, iCloud sync catches up when you reconnect.

Apple Reminders & Calendar

Where do my items go — WhisperAct or Apple’s apps?

Each capture has a destination, shown at the top of the review screen. With Apple apps selected — the default — reminders are saved to Apple Reminders and events to Apple Calendar. To-dos stay in WhisperAct unless you turn on Sync todos to Apple Reminders in Settings.

Choose WhisperAct instead and the items stay in the app; you can send any of them to Apple later from the item’s menu. You can change the default destination in Settings → Apple apps.

Why aren't my tasks showing up in Apple Reminders or Calendar?

First, check the destination: items saved to WhisperAct stay in the app until you sync them, and to-dos stay in WhisperAct unless Sync todos to Apple Reminders is on.

On the free plan you can sync 7 items to Apple Reminders and 7 to Apple Calendar each month — two separate allowances that reset on the 1st. When one runs out, items of that type are kept in WhisperAct and you can sync them later from the item’s menu. Pro has no limit.

WhisperAct also needs Full Access to Reminders and Calendars: in the iPhone Settings app, go to Privacy & Security → Reminders (or Calendars) and check that WhisperAct is set to Full Access.

Can I review items before they're saved to Apple Reminders or Calendar?

Yes — by default every capture opens for review first. You can edit the title, switch an item between to-do, reminder and event, change the date, add an item it missed, or leave one out. If a date is vague, WhisperAct asks which day you meant. Nothing is written to Apple Reminders or Apple Calendar until you confirm.

After you’ve reviewed several captures, WhisperAct offers auto-save for clear items, so confident captures save straight away while anything unclear still opens for review. You can turn it on or off any time in Settings → Apple apps → Auto-save clear items.

How many free Apple syncs do I get?

The free plan includes 7 syncs to Apple Reminders and 7 syncs to Apple Calendar every month — tracked separately, so up to 14 Apple items in total. Each new reminder or event created in Apple uses one sync from its own allowance; editing an item that’s already in Apple doesn’t. Both reset at the start of each month.

Pro has unlimited syncs. You can check your usage any time in Settings → Sync & Usage.

How do I set a location-based reminder?

Say something like “remind me to grab my prescription when I get to the pharmacy” and WhisperAct will offer a place instead of a time. You can also tap any reminder, choose “When I arrive or leave”, search for the place and set how close you need to be.

Location reminders are a Pro feature. They are delivered through Apple Reminders, so they fire even when WhisperAct is closed. The alert may not appear the instant you arrive.

Can WhisperAct create repeating reminders?

Yes, and it is available on the free plan. Say “take my vitamins every morning at eight” or “submit my timesheet every Friday” and WhisperAct asks you to confirm the repeat, sets the first occurrence and rolls it forward each time you complete it. You can also add or edit a repeat manually on any dated item.

For items synced to Apple, Pro additionally writes the repeat as a native recurring rule in Apple Reminders and Calendar, so it stays in sync there too.

Plans & purchases

What does Pro include, and what is Lifetime?

Pro removes the free limits — recordings up to 20 minutes, unlimited Apple syncs and Re-Analyze, and your full memo history — and adds iCloud sync across your iPhone and iPad, location reminders, a specific Reminders list or calendar of your choice, import from Apple Reminders, PDF and bulk export, and premium themes.

Pro Monthly, Pro Yearly and Pro Lifetime all unlock the same features; Lifetime is a one-time purchase that never renews. On the US App Store they cost $2.99 a month, $24.99 a year or $99.99 once, as of October 21, 2026. The App Store shows the price for your country or region before you buy.

What is Re-Analyze and when does it appear?

Re-Analyze runs a memo’s saved transcript through Cloud AI again, without re-recording — useful when a memo was processed offline or on your device. Open the memo: when Re-Analyze is available, it appears at the top. It needs an internet connection.

The free plan includes 7 Re-Analyses a month and Pro is unlimited. If a typed memo, or one recorded in a language other than English, came back with no items, a recovery Re-Analyze is offered that doesn’t count toward the monthly limit.

How do I access my older memos?

The free plan shows your 7 most recent memos. Older memos stay on your device but appear locked, and upgrading to Pro unlocks your full history without any data loss. On the free plan, memos are stored only on this device, so deleting the app removes them.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone → tap your name → Subscriptions → WhisperAct → Cancel Subscription. In WhisperAct you can also open Settings and tap Manage Subscription on your plan. Pro stays active until the end of the period you’ve paid for. Pro Lifetime is a one-time purchase, so there’s nothing to cancel.

I lost my purchase after reinstalling. How do I restore?

Open WhisperAct, go to Settings and tap Restore Purchases (it’s also on the upgrade screen). Your purchase is restored as long as you’re signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it. If that doesn’t work, please contact us.

Privacy & your data

Is my voice data private?

WhisperAct has no account system and no servers of its own for your memos. English recordings are transcribed by Apple’s speech recognition — on-device on supported iOS 26 devices, otherwise by Apple’s speech servers, which WhisperAct doesn’t see. Recordings in other languages are sent to Groq for transcription; WhisperAct has turned on Groq’s Zero Data Retention setting. The recording itself is deleted once it has been transcribed.

When you’re online, the text of your memo — not the audio — is sent to OpenAI to find tasks, reminders and events, with Cerebras as a backup. These requests don’t include your name or Apple ID, but they do include what you said or typed. OpenAI may keep abuse-monitoring logs for up to 30 days; Cerebras states that it doesn’t retain requests. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

How do I delete all my data?

Go to Settings inside WhisperAct and tap “Delete All Data”. This permanently removes all memos and tasks from your device and iCloud. This action cannot be undone.
