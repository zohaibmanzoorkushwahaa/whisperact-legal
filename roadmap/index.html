Roadmap

The voyage so far, and the course ahead.

WhisperAct set sail on the App Store on June 18, 2026. This map follows every checkpoint since: what each update brought, where the app is today, and the leg that’s being prepared right now.

Leg 01 · June 2026

Setting sail

The app launched with the whole loop in place: speak, review, save to Apple’s apps.

  1. June 18, 20261.1.0

    WhisperAct lands on the App Store

    Speak a memo and it becomes reminders in Apple Reminders, events in Apple Calendar and to-dos in the app. Capture from anywhere: Siri, the Action Button, Control Center and widgets.

    • iPhone
    • iPad
    • Free + Pro
  2. June 221.2.1

    Type it or speak it

    Typed memos in any language, sorted the same way as spoken ones. Review before anything saves: fix a type or date, add what was missed. To-dos appear on the widget.

  3. June 25 – July 21.3.0 – 1.3.4

    Sturdier foundations

    Separate Apple sync allowances for Reminders and Calendar. Upgraded Cloud AI with notes on items, duplicate-reminder detection and clearer conflict choices. Japanese recording. A destination setting for Apple apps or WhisperAct, plus a status widget with its own themes.

Leg 02 · July 2026

Finding the way

Six updates in one month: a new Plan, more languages, repeats you can just say.

  1. July 41.4.1

    Pro themes

    Royal Blue and Forest join the original Warm Paper look, with smoother previews and better small-screen support for recording.

    • Pro
  2. July 9 – 101.5.0 – 1.5.1

    A plan you can read

    Plan is rebuilt around Today, Upcoming and Inbox, with search and a clearer Next Up. Daily Review arrives to clear overdue and undecided items in a quick morning pass.

  3. July 141.6.0

    15 languages, two interfaces

    Recording grows to 15 languages, from Spanish and French to Korean and Japanese, alongside English, Urdu and Arabic. The app itself speaks Spanish as well as English.

  4. July 171.7.0

    Quick Add and Apple Reminders import

    Add a task, reminder or event by hand from Plan, no memo needed. Pro brings incomplete reminders in from chosen Apple Reminders lists. Settings reorganized into clear sections.

    • Free
    • Pro: import
  5. July 2026Product Hunt

    Hello, Product Hunt

    WhisperAct launched to the Product Hunt community. Early comments praised voice-to-tasks and asked for two things, a one-tap record widget and location reminders. Both have since shipped.

    Read the launch
  6. July 222.0.0

    Repeats, spoken

    “Take my medicine every morning at 8” sets the schedule and the repeat. Mark one done and the next is waiting. Custom repeats from a quick picker; vague timings get a question, not a guess. Free for everyone; Pro also writes native series into Apple’s apps.

    • Free
    • Pro: native Apple series
  7. July 26 – 312.0.1 – 2.1.0

    Steadier, and in your language

    Skip one occurrence of a repeat without touching the rest. Titles, notes and places stay in the language you recorded in. A quiet iCloud status icon shows up only when sync actually needs attention.

Leg 03 · August 2026

New territory

Reminders learn where you are, and the app learns when your day happens.

  1. August 9 – 143.0.0 – 3.0.1

    Reminders that know where you are

    “Grab stamps when I get to the post office” becomes a real Apple Reminders location alert. A full-screen place picker with map search, radius, arrive and leave, plus Saved Places for the spots you use most.

    • Pro
  2. August 213.0.2

    Personal or Work

    Tag a memo as Personal or Work before you speak, then filter Memos and Plan by it. Running out of storage now shows clear recovery steps instead of losing work.

  3. August 243.1.0

    Your morning, your way

    Time Preferences: say what morning, afternoon and evening mean to you, when your day starts and ends, and how long an event is when you don’t say. “Morning” and “evening” now work in every recording language. Pro members get a Plan & Billing page.

Leg 04 · September 2026

A calmer day

A gentler first recording, repeats you confirm, and a whole tab for your day.

  1. September 1 – 83.1.1 – 3.1.2

    Clearer from the first recording

    A smoother first run with examples and clearer permission guidance. Active and Done memos grouped by date. A repeat heard in your words is shown for a quick confirm before it’s saved, and items always land in the list or calendar you expect. Larger, easier text on iPad.

  2. September 183.2.0

    Meet the Day tab

    A calm home for your day replaces the Usage tab. Drag markers on a timeline to shape your day, see the exact time each phrase turns into, set Daily Review and evening check-ins, and watch a live sky that follows your day. Daily Review gains Back, Skip and a clearer summary.

Leg 05 · October 2026

Where we are

The current App Store release.

  1. You are hereOctober 11, 20264.0.0

    Room to work on iPad, and Pro for life

    Smarter handling of dates, times, repeats and multi-step instructions, with clearer questions when something is uncertain. iPad gets landscape and Split View layouts, keyboard shortcuts, pointer support and separate windows. A Lifetime option joins the monthly and yearly plans.

    • Scheduling follows your preferred times, including days that end after midnight.
    • Easier completion and rescheduling: bigger checkboxes, steadier rows, clearer Undo, and quick date choices from a long-press in Plan.
    • More reliable Apple Reminders sync for repeating reminders.
    • iPhone
    • iPad
    • Pro Lifetime
